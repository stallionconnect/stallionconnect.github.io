Six new directions for the logo, all based on the current one and shown in a range of colours. Have a look, pick a favourite design and colour scheme, and tell us what you would change. Nothing here is final.

How to reply

Just send us a message with your answers to these:

  1. Which option do you like most?A first and second choice is even better, for example "Option 3, then Option 1".
  2. Which colours do you like?Use the palette letter, for example "Option 3 in colours C", or say if you'd rather keep the current navy and green.
  3. What would you change?Font, icon, spacing: anything that doesn't feel right.
  4. Would you like to combine options?For example the icon from one option with the text style of another.
